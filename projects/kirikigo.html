GAME DEVELOPMENT

KirikiGo

2D Precision Platformer

Introduction

KirikiGo is a 2D precision platformer where you need to grab an apple to complete each level. You control a hedgehog who can transform into different animals to move through the terrain.

This game was originally created for the first Euskal Game Jam, a Game Jam organized in collaboration with the university UPV/EHU, with the goal of making games in Euskera, the language of the Basque Country.

While studying for my Master's degree, I remade the game using a custom engine in C++, based on the Raylib graphics library.

About the game

In this game, you play as a hedgehog who wants to eat apples. Each apple is found in a different level, and you need to reach it to complete the level.

The hedgehog can transform into different animals, each with its own abilities, to move through the terrain and reach the apple. As a hedgehog, you move fast and can pop bubbles. As a frog you can jump and as a bird you can fly.

To transform, you need to touch a spirit, and it will be kept in reserve until you transform into it. In the original game, the spirits weren't visually different from the player, so in the new version they appear with a blue tint.

Another difference between the original and the remake is the player's speed. The first one is faster and has slippery physics, which make it more difficult to control, especially in some levels where you need precision. In the remake, the player moves more slowly, allowing for better control.
