My Contributions

Camera System

Developed the camera system that follows the player and allows for cinematic camera movements during cutscenes. It also includes camera areas which modify the camera position in certan areas, for example, a fixed camera for a puzzle.

Interactuables and events

Designed and implemented a system to easily connect interactuables and events in the game. The game has interactuables such as buttons, levers, lifts, trapdoors, and pressure plates that send generic events and any other actor can listen to those events and react accordingly thanks to a special component. I also implemented the buttons, levers, lifts, trapdoors, and pressure plates in the game.

Hook System

In the house there is a hook that allows to grab objects and move them around. This system can move any actor who has a grabbable component.

Narrator Prototype

During the game, a narrator is present to tell the story. I implemented a prototype of this narrator to play audios and subtitles in sync, according to data assets.
