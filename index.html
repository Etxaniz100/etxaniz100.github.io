About Me

I have always loved being able to turn my ideas into something tangible and programming has been the perfect outlet for this. I studied a Bachelor's Degree in Computer Engineering in Management and Information Systems and continued my education with a Master’s Degree in Game Development, Computer Games and Programming Skills.

In my Bachelor's Degree, I learned about Mathematics, Databases, Software Architecture and AI, among many other subjects. With all this, I started to delve deeper into the game development world and creating simple games for fun. Finally, for my Bachelor's Thesis I decided to go for it and make a more complex game, earning me the highest possible grade with honors.

After that, I decided this was my career path and pursued a Master’s Degree in Game Development. During my Master's, I have learned about and work with:

- Unreal Engine 5, C++ and Blueprints

- Unity and C#

- More mathematics (games are all mathematics)

- Videogame AI and game development automation

- And making games our own custom engines, using graphics libraries such as OpenGL and Raylib

Right now I am working on my Master’s Thesis, Unstrung, a puzzle-based lateral scrolling game. We are a team of 35+ people, and managing such a team has been quite a challenge, but also a rewarding experience.
